# Product

QUOTA: anonymous, deposit-backed rate limits for AI-agent traffic on Monad. A primitive (contracts, SDKs, middleware) with a small web surface: landing, operator console, service console, live demo, docs.

## Audience
- Service operators (MCP hosts, free data/search APIs) who must stop abuse without accounts.
- Agent operators who want agents to call many services without leaving a trail.
- Hackathon judges (Monad Metropolis, Trust, Identity & AI Infrastructure) scanning for a real primitive.

## Voice
Plain, concrete, honest about limits. No hype verbs. State what is confirmed vs deferred (BTX unavailable, Cleanverse dropped, Qwen deferred, Dynamic partial).

## Brand commitments
- Transit-signage world: the product's journey is a metro line (Stake, Prove, Check, Slash). Cool concrete ground, ink, cobalt line, signal red for slashing, green for verified.
- Demo data is always labelled synthetic until the backend lands.

## Assumptions (inferred, no interview)
Visual world was pinned by the request (scroll-driven flight, anti-slop). Mode per surface: landing = Persuade, consoles = Operate, docs = Read, demo = Experience.
